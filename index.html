Our work

We make fewer things, properly.

Bargoni started with one bench and a stack of vegetable-tanned hide. We still cut to order in small runs, which is why a style sometimes sells out before we restock it, and why the leather on a two-year-old bag looks better than it did on the day it left us.

Dress shoes are Goodyear welted so the sole can be replaced. Bags are edge-painted and hand-burnished, with solid brass hardware rather than plated. Nothing here is glued together to hit a price.

If you are between sizes, or not sure whether a bag will take your laptop, message us before you order. We would rather answer a question than process a return.
